Inter Cellular Exchange

Exclusive technological formula composed of unicellular freshwater microalgae and selected beneficial bacteria. Microscopic living cells that store nutrients in biologically active form, stabilize natural auxins and cytokinins, and rapidly penetrate foliar stomata to transfer their plasma into depleted plant cells.

Essential Conditions for Cytoplasmic Transmission

[ SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS 01 • 02 • 03 ]
01

Functional Connection: Plasmodesmata

Requires a contiguous connection system between two cells: the plasmodesmata (interconnected strands of continuous cytoplasm traversing plant cell walls).

Link: Permeable membrane & cellular microchannels
02

Diluted Hypoosmotic Medium

A surrounding environment of lower concentration than the cellular interior (the spongy intercellular space of the leaf or the edaphic freshwater solution).

Environment: Hypoosmotic without saline saturation
03

Concentration Differential

A marked gradient between the Agroplasma I.C.E. cell (densely loaded with bioactive nutrients) and the recipient plant cell (depleted or in active growth phase).

Rule: [Agroplasma Solutes] >> [Plant Cell]
System Note: I.C.E. cellular exchange exhibits its highest metabolic activity in young, actively developing tissues, particularly in leaves and root tips.

[ SYSTEM PROCESS 02 ] RHIZOSPHERE & SOIL

Soil Transfer & Mineral Unlocking

Diagram: Phosphorus & Calcium Unlocking in Rhizosphere File: img/tree root.png

Agroplasma I.C.E. cells applied to the soil actively unlock previously fixed fertilizers and minerals in the mineral horizon (topsoil/subsoil).

The process initiates water diffusion from the root cell toward the Agroplasma cell. The nutrient-loaded algal cell then transports cytoplasmic material via continuous mass flow into the plant.

Once equilibrium is reached, the flow ceases: the biodegradable cell envelope dissolves naturally, releasing its trace elements for active cellular uptake.

Residue: 0% saline • Higher efficiency of existing soil fertilization.
[ LINEAR USER-FLOW ]

Cytoplasmic Current: In Search of Homeostatic Equilibrium

Biological rule: Nutrients always flow from zones of high concentration toward zones of lower concentration until physiological balance is achieved.

STEP 01 • DONOR
Agroplasma I.C.E. Cells

Dense cellular concentration of live freshwater algae, auxins, cytokinins, and biologically chelated minerals.

STEP 02 • CHANNEL
Plasmodesmata

Continuous intercellular bridges allowing direct passage of macromolecules and solutes without ATP expenditure.

STEP 03 • RECIPIENT
Plant Cells

Foliar or root cells with lower concentration that instantaneously receive the enriched plasma.

STEP 04 • DISPATCH
Plant-Wide Distribution

Rapid translocation to storage organs, budding, fruit set, sizing, and immediate cellular regeneration.
